The Problem
Every wellness app
owns your data.

Whoop. Fitbit. Oura. Apple Watch. AI is being embedded into every fitness and wellness platform on the market. They are all, without exception, cloud-dependent. Your biometric data is processed on servers you don't control, under terms of service written to maximize the company's options for that data.

The customer is already here

The Solution
Local inference.
Real sovereignty.

The Spark is a wellness coaching and habit-tracking application that runs entirely on WCN-controlled infrastructure. When a user submits an intake, the data travels over an encrypted Cloudflare tunnel, is processed on our hardware, and the result is returned. At no point does it touch OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any third-party AI provider.

The Research
Dissertation-grade
validation built in.

The 100 Days of Summer study is a Drexel IRB-approved randomized controlled trial examining whether AI-personalized behavioral nudges improve habit adherence over 100 days — and whether personalization provides incremental benefit beyond generic nudging.

1
Control — platform access, in-app Coach's Note, no push notifications
2
Generic nudge — standardized push notifications, identical across group
3
AI-personalized nudge — notifications generated from participant's own logged behavioral data
NSF SBIR eligibility

This study structure is the feasibility documentation NSF Phase I requires. DH5 — Physical, Mental and Behavioral Health. $275K non-dilutive. No match requirement. Project Pitch deadline July 27, 2026.
