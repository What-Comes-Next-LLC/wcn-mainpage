Transformation

Program-level objective

Name the goal and how long you are giving it. Choose up to three things you will actually do, and which days. Not ten. Three.

  • Note to Self, on your phone Being builtGoal, length, three habits and their days. No account needed.
  • A Day In The Life LiveTell it your day and your goal; it hands back three cards for the next six weeks, made from what you said.
2

Evolution

A fixed-length sprint, planned on Sunday

One week at a time. Pick this week’s focus, the one change that makes the rest easier, and put each habit on the days it can really happen.

  • Plan the week Being builtThe Sunday screen in Note to Self.
  • Sword Sharpening In useThe founder’s own weekly planning, kept for years before there was a company.
3

Every day

Execution, and the record of it

Check off what you did. Write a line about it. When a sentence says something true, long-press it and keep it as a Note to Self. It waits for Saturday, word for word.

  • Today, on your phone Being builtChecks, the week’s dots, your entries, Notes to Self in violet.
  • The Keeper, on your laptop Being builtA binder: one folder per Transformation, everything you kept for it in one place, the open folder the only thing in view.
4

Look Back

Retrospective

Saturday. What you said you would do, next to what you did, and the sentences you kept. No grade. Feelings first, then the record, then three things worth remembering.

  • Your week, on your phone Being builtCounts, the grid, your Notes to Self, and one button to send it.
  • Look Back with the Coach LiveYour week, talked through on a ten-minute call. Ten dollars and six cents.

What Comes Next?

The next sprint, re-planned from the retrospective

Keep what worked, change one thing, and go again. A Transformation is a run of these. The question in the company’s name is the step that makes it a loop instead of a list.

  • Note to Self NextThe whole loop, on your phone and your laptop, for anyone. Splat is its face. Your record stays yours: nobody looks unless you show them.
