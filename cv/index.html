Current research

100 Days of Summer Aug 10 – Nov 18, 2026

A 100-day minimal-risk field experiment on AI-personalized habit feedback, under Drexel University IRB review (HRP-504, study 2605011824). Dissertation chair: Dr. Mark Stehr, Director, School of Economics, Drexel LeBow.

I designed the protocol, built the intervention, and operate the infrastructure it runs on. The system delivers personalized behavioral feedback from a locally hosted language model — no cloud inference, no third-party processing, and no participant health data leaving hardware I control. The instrument is a React Native application; the analysis layer runs retrieval over longitudinal, participant-authored text.
