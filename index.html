The methodology

Evolutions — personal project management.

Health practitioners do not do project management. Project managers are not known for being healthy and fit. The intersection is nearly empty.

Evolutions is twenty years of portfolio-scale program discipline — 500+ application migrations, 4,600+ data-source integrations, a $2M statewide modernization delivered 14% under budget — pointed at a single person instead of an enterprise. Not a metaphor. The same constructs, renamed.

The product

Your own data, read back to you.

A Day In The Life

One-off purchase · No subscription · In development

If you have two years of Garmin, Hevy, or Apple Health history sitting in an export you have never opened, that is already a longitudinal record of how you actually live. A Day In The Life reads it and tells you what it says.

  • Bring your own export — no new tracking, no new app to remember. Not built yet
  • The report is generated on hardware we own and operate — no cloud model, no third-party processing.
  • We store what was derived, never the file you uploaded. The PDF is built in memory, sent, and gone.
  • Sparse input earns a tight report; a rich history earns a fuller one. Sections are earned, not padded.

Not a purchase and not a charge. Importing your own data is designed but not built — today this is the list that gets told first, and what you pick decides which export format gets built first. If you want to start now, The Spark is live and free.
