The methodology

Evolutions — personal project management.

Health practitioners do not do project management. Project managers are not known for being healthy and fit. The intersection is nearly empty.

Evolutions is twenty years of portfolio-scale program discipline — 500+ application migrations, 4,600+ data-source integrations, a $2M statewide modernization delivered 14% under budget — pointed at a single person instead of an enterprise. Not a metaphor. The same constructs, renamed.

Every term is a project-management construct applied to a person

The Future of Work, Revisited

For PMOs, PMI chapters, and project leadership

In 2024 I told PMI Great Lakes that the PMO should be the tip of the spear for AI in the organization, because a good PMO knows where the wins are and where the work is. Two years later, AI is reshaping project organizations whether or not the PMO leads it.

This is the follow-up: where AI earns its keep in project delivery, where it doesn’t, and what a PMO that intends to still exist in 2028 does next.

About

Jason Rashaad

Doctoral researcher studying how AI-personalized feedback changes habit formation — on a system he built, and ran on himself first.

DBA candidate at Drexel University’s LeBow College of Business. Before that, twenty years of portfolio-scale technical program management at General Motors, Ford, Amazon, and the State of Michigan, and federal compliance work at Booz Allen, Oracle, and Deloitte.

What Comes Next? was built out of a practice that already existed — daily progress files, weekly planning sessions, retrospectives — kept for years before there was a company. The personal practice and the platform converged on the same structure because the same person built both.
