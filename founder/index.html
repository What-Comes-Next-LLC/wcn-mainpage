Founder · Jason Rashaad

Twenty years. Six times without anywhere to go.

I started this company because no one hires anyone with this in their history. So here it is: my record, as counts, from my own exports. One row per month, starting in 2015.

The people

Each thread is a person. Lit, we talked that month; brighter, we talked more. Faint, the thread is still there and we weren’t talking. Tap a thread to follow it.

 

A conversation ends after six quiet hours. Threads are people I had three or more conversations with since 2015, over one-to-one texts, Google Voice, Gchat and Bluesky; no names, ever. Dark grey months have no record at all, which is not the same as silence.

The places

Nineteen countries, in the order I first went.

Every photo I took with a place on it, as a glow, and a flag for every country. Swipe the globe sideways; scrub the trip below it.
