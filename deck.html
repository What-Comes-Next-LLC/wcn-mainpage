The Research
Dissertation-grade
validation built in.

The 100 Days of Summer study is a randomized controlled trial under Drexel IRB review. Three conditions. 100 days. Does AI personalization improve habit adherence beyond generic nudging?

1
Control — platform access, in-app Coach's Note, no push notifications
2
Generic nudge — standardized push notifications, identical across group
3
AI-personalized nudge — notifications generated from participant's own logged behavioral data
NSF SBIR DH5 eligible

This study structure qualifies as feasibility documentation for an NSF Phase I application. Non-dilutive funding path active in parallel.
